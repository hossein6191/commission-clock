Quiz night at Tidepool Cafe On the first Thursday of every month, from 7 pm, the tables are pushed together and the cafe becomes a quiz room. Teams of up to six play six rounds: general knowledge, music, the sea, pictures, a mystery round, and a final round where every point counts double. Entry is free and there is no need to book. Just turn up, give your team a name and pick up a pencil. The winners take home a hamper and see their team name written up by the door until the next quiz. The kitchen stays open until the last round, and the quizmaster's decision is final, even when it is wrong.
