This teapot from Fernhill Pottery is thrown by hand from stoneware clay and holds one litre, enough to pour four good cups. The spout is cut clean at the tip, so it pours without dripping, and the lid has a deep flange that stays in place while you tip the pot. Inside, a removable stainless steel infuser takes loose leaf tea and lifts out once the tea has brewed. The handle is wide and pulled by hand, with space for your whole hand, and it stays cool to hold. We finish the teapot in our oatmeal glaze, a warm speckled cream that shows the marks of the wheel. The base is left unglazed and sits flat on any table. Fired to a high temperature, it is strong enough for daily use, and it is dishwasher safe once the infuser is taken out. Each teapot is made by hand, so small variations are part of its character.
