Weekend baker wanted at Larkrise Bakery We are looking for an early riser to join our small team as a weekend baker. The shifts run from 4 am to noon on Saturdays and Sundays, shaping and baking our breads, buns and pastries alongside our head baker. You do not need years of experience, but you should enjoy working with your hands, keep a tidy bench and be happy to start before sunrise. We will teach you everything else, from mixing a dough to reading the oven. The job pays a fair hourly rate, with bread to take home at the end of every shift. To apply, leave a short note about yourself at the shop, or ask for the head baker at the counter.
