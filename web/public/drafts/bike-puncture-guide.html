How to fix a puncture First, take the wheel off the bike and let out any air that is left. Use two tyre levers to ease one side of the tyre off the rim, then pull out the inner tube. Pump a little air into the tube and listen for the hiss, or hold it under water and watch for bubbles. Mark the hole, rub the rubber around it with sandpaper, and spread a thin layer of glue. Wait a minute until it is tacky, then press the patch on firmly. Run your fingers carefully around the inside of the tyre to find whatever caused the puncture, and take it out. Put the tube back, push the tyre onto the rim with your thumbs, pump it up and refit the wheel.
