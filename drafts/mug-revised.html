Every mug from Fernhill Pottery begins as a lump of stoneware clay on the wheel and is shaped by hand, so no two are quite the same. This one holds 350 ml, enough for a generous cup of tea or a proper morning coffee. The walls are thrown a little thicker at the base, which keeps the mug steady on the table and your drink warm for longer. The handle is pulled by hand and leaves room for three fingers. We finish it in our sea-green glaze, a soft colour that pools darker where the clay curves and turns almost blue at the rim. The foot is left unglazed.
