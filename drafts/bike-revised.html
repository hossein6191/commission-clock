Spokes & Sprockets has been fixing bikes on the same corner for fifteen years, since we first opened our doors in the spring of 2011. What started as one workbench and a box of spanners is now a busy little workshop, but we still work the same way: we look at your bike with you, tell you plainly what it needs, and never fix what is not broken. From punctures and brake tuning to full rebuilds of much-loved old frames, every job gets the same care, and we fix wheelchairs and prams too. Most repairs are ready the next day. Pop in, say hello, and bring your bike along.
